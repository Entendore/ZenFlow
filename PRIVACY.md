# ZenFlow Privacy Policy

ZenFlow is a fully offline meditation app.

- It **collects no data** and **transmits nothing** — it has no internet
  permission at all (verifiable in the app manifest).
- It contains **no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs**.
- Sessions, presets, favorites, and settings exist **only on your device**
  and are never backed up to cloud services by the app.
- Imported audio files are copied into the app's private storage and never
  leave the device.
- Deleting the app deletes everything.
